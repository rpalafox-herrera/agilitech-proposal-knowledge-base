# Fictional demonstration projects

**Every proposal, year, quantity, budget, schedule and labor figure in this dataset is invented for a university project. These are not Agilitech project records, quotations, results or company statistics.** Client names are deliberately blank. Nothing here is an engineering recommendation or a validated estimate.

The industry and service vocabulary is inspired by Agilitech's public website. Its [renewable energy page](https://www.agilitechgroup.com/our-industries/renewable-energy/) describes solar, storage, microgrids and EV charging; its [water and wastewater page](https://www.agilitechgroup.com/our-industries/water-waste-water/) describes treatment services. Other relevant sources are [food and beverage](https://www.agilitechgroup.com/our-industries/food-and-beverage/), [manufacturing](https://www.agilitechgroup.com/our-industries/manufacturing/), [mining](https://www.agilitechgroup.com/our-industries/mining/), [construction](https://www.agilitechgroup.com/our-services/electrical-construction/), [engineering](https://www.agilitechgroup.com/our-services/engineering/), and [automation](https://www.agilitechgroup.com/our-services/control-system-automation-and-software-development/). These pages support the categories only; **none supplies the fictional numbers below**. Public pages reviewed November 9, 2026.

The table above shows the original ten fictional teaching examples. The shipped archive contains **34 fictional proposals**: the ten original examples plus four added examples for each of six industries. This keeps each workspace populated while remaining easy to browse. Its additional filenames begin with `fictional_archive_`.

All expanded-archive amounts, quantities, schedules and labor estimates are generated classroom assumptions. They are demo dataset statistics only, never real company metrics. The inclusive year range is chosen to exercise about twenty years of filtering; it does not establish any company's operating history. The dashboard shows fictional figures only inside the selected industry workspace, so they are never mixed with uploaded documents or presented as cross-company totals.

`sample_content.py` contains the source records. The original searchable wording includes `water recovery`, `industrial electrical construction`, and `solar installation` so these example searches return useful results. Quoted phrases can exercise phrase search; metadata can exercise industry, year, discipline and project-type filters. All fictional figures also appear in each document's body, allowing content searches such as `1 MWh` or `185,000`.

Show the **FICTIONAL UNIVERSITY DEMO** label whenever these projects or figures appear in a presentation or app. Do not describe planned assumptions as achieved results or combine demo budget totals with real business records.
